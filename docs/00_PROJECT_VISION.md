# MedFinder

## Product Vision & Development Roadmap

**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 14 July 2026

---

# 1. Executive Summary

MedFinder is a healthcare technology startup focused on solving one of the most common yet overlooked healthcare problems: **the difficulty of locating medicines in real time across nearby pharmacies**.

Instead of visiting multiple pharmacies or making numerous phone calls, users can search for a medicine and instantly view its availability, price (future), nearby pharmacies, and navigation directions within seconds.

MedFinder aims to become India's most trusted real-time medicine discovery platform while creating a connected digital ecosystem between pharmacies and customers.

---

# 2. Vision Statement

> **To simplify medicine accessibility through technology and become the most reliable healthcare search platform in India.**

---

# 3. Mission

To connect patients and pharmacies through a secure, intelligent, and user-friendly digital platform that reduces medicine search time and improves healthcare accessibility.

---

# 4. Problem Statement

Today, patients often face challenges such as:

* Visiting multiple pharmacies before finding required medicines.
* Calling pharmacies individually.
* Difficulty finding emergency medicines at night.
* No centralized real-time inventory platform.
* Lack of digital visibility for local pharmacies.

These issues become even more critical during medical emergencies.
